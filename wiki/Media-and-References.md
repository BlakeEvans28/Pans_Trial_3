## Screenshot Checklist

The written report identifies the screenshots that best explain the flow. These are the recommended captures for a polished public wiki or release page:

- Main menu with title art, Start Game, Two Player, How To Play, Settings, and Quit.
- Coin flip screen showing the resolved draft order.
- Draft screen with the public 12-card pool and both players' developing hands.
- Omen reveal screen showing all four Jack assignments.
- Traversing board with both players, health-left HUD, Omen legend, and holes.
- Appeasing Pan result with both submitted cards and role-strength guidance.
- Pan request chooser showing Restructure, Steal Life, Ignore Us, and Plane Shift.
- Plane Shift selection with a highlighted row or column.
- Restructure selection with the two suit families selected.
- Steal Life selection with both damage piles visible.
- Game-over screen with winner, final health, match summary, Play Again, and Main Menu.
- Two Player room flow with a visible room code or joined player.
